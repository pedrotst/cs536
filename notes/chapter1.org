** A _Switch_ stores & forwards data
** A switched networks can be of two forms
*** Circuit Switch
**** Telephone System
**** It streams the data
**** Does not store the date
*** Pack Switch
**** sends discrete blocks of data
***** aka packet or message

* Convention

* A node conected to two ore more networks is called a _router_ or _gateway_

* Q: How do we know how to find the recipient of a message
* A: By assigning addresses
* The procedure of deciding how to forward messages until it reatches its address is called routing
  
* Q: How can hosts share the network?
* A: Multiplexing
** like timesharing in the OS level
** Synchronous time-division multiplexing (STDM)
*** Round Robing
*** In quantum 1, data from 1st flow is transfered
*** In quantum 2, data from 2nd flow is transfered
*** and so on...
*** Con: if no data is sent from flow x, then quantum x is wasted
** Frequency Division Multiplexing (FDM)
*** transmit on different frequencies
*** Not practical to add new frequencies
** Statistical Multiplexing
*** Basically STDM but solving the "no data" problem
*** It only transfers when a node asks for a timeslice
*** Keeps the use fair by setting a maximum data limit
**** this data limit is called a _packet_
